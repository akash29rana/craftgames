Kitten Word

Connect words. Discover stories. Enjoy a relaxing word puzzle adventure. Kitten Word is free to download on Google Play and the App Store. Both listings also say it contains ads and in-app purchases.

Play the browser demo

What you play

The pictures on this site were drawn for the website. They are not screenshots of the app.

Word Connect

Swipe across letters and form words. There is no timer. Try a short version in the browser demo.

Word Search

Find hidden words in the grid. A small three-level version is in the word search demo.

Cat stories

The Play description says every level you solve unlocks a heartwarming cat story. See the story titles.

A long trail

Google Play lists 1,000+ levels, daily rewards, and hints if you get stuck. It also says you can play offline.

Good to know

Is Kitten Word free?

It is free to download. The Google Play listing says the app contains ads and offers in-app purchases.

Can I play offline?

The store description says you can play offline, anywhere, anytime.

Are the puzzles timed?

The store description says there are no timers and no pressure.

Who do I write to for help?

Email info@craftgames.in. That is the support address on the Play listing.

What ages is it for?

Google Play rates Kitten Word Everyone, and the description calls it great for all ages.
