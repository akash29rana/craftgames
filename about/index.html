About Us

A small studio making games that feel like a warm pause in the day.

Craft Games is an independent game studio focused on creating delightful mobile gaming experiences. We combine thoughtful game design, creative storytelling and engaging puzzles to build games that bring joy to players.

Our vision

We want a few quiet minutes with a game to leave someone smiling. The puzzles should feel clear, the worlds should feel cozy, and the stories should be worth staying for.

Our approach

We design gently. Rules stay easy to learn, the art stays warm, and stories unfold as you play. Kitten Word is our featured game. This website is set up so more games can be added beside it later.

How we think about quality

We care about the feel of a level and the warmth of the characters. Kitten Word is free to download on Google Play and the App Store. The Google Play listing says the puzzles have no timers, that the game can be played offline, and that it contains ads and in-app purchases.
