Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

This page describes the Craft Games website, and it repeats what the public Google Play Data safety section for Kitten Word said on 3 November 2026. It does not name advertising, analytics, or payment providers, because those names have not been confirmed. Paragraphs marked Needs confirmation should be checked before this page is treated as a complete policy.

Who we are

Craft Games publishes this website at craftgames.in and the mobile game Kitten Word. You can write to info@craftgames.in.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It does not ask you to create an account. It does not run a contact form, website analytics, or its own advertising cookies. The fonts are stored with the site, so opening a page does not call a separate font service.

Contact links open your own email app. You send the message yourself to info@craftgames.in, and the message contains whatever you choose to write.

The site is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages. A host can process technical connection data, such as an IP address, to deliver the pages. Needs confirmation if the host changes.

Information the Kitten Word app may collect

On 3 November 2026, the Google Play listing for Kitten Word (package name com.superfun.wordlotus) showed the following in its Data safety section:

  • The app may collect app activity and device or other IDs.
  • The developer declared that no data is shared with third parties.
  • Data is encrypted in transit.
  • The form says data can’t be deleted.

The same listing says the app contains ads and offers in-app purchases.

Needs confirmation: the names of any advertising networks, analytics tools, crash reporters, or payment processors used inside the app. Those names are not in the Data safety summary this page relies on.

Cookies and website analytics

This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies, so it does not show a cookie banner. Needs confirmation if analytics or other cookies are added later. This page should be updated at the same time.

Third-party services

Links to Google Play and the App Store leave this website and are covered by Google’s and Apple’s own terms and privacy policies. The data-safety details above are from the Google Play listing. Needs confirmation: the iOS privacy details, and any other third-party services used inside the app.

Advertising

The Play listing says Kitten Word contains ads. This website does not show ads. Needs confirmation: which ad networks the app uses, and whether those ads are personalized.

In-app purchases

The Play listing says Kitten Word offers in-app purchases. This website does not sell anything. Needs confirmation: who processes those payments, and what purchase records are kept.

Children’s privacy

Google Play rates Kitten Word Everyone, and the store description says the game is for all ages. This website does not ask visitors to make an account or fill in a form. Needs confirmation: whether the app is directed to children, whether an age gate is used, and how parental consent is handled. Do not treat this section as a complete children’s privacy notice until that is confirmed.

Data retention

The Data safety summary reviewed for this page does not say how long app data is kept. Needs confirmation.

Data deletion

The Play Data safety label reviewed on 3 November 2026 says data can’t be deleted. If you have a question about the website or the app, email info@craftgames.in. Needs confirmation: whether any in-app deletion path exists, because the public label says deletion is not available.

Changes

We may update this page. When we do, the date at the top will change.
